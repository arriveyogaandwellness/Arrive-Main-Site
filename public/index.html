Proprietary Framework

The Arrive Yoga Method™

This isn't performative yoga. No flexibility contest. No comparison. It’s a guided, trauma-aware, nervous-system supportive return to yourself, accessible for every single body.

A

ANCHOR

Safety • Breath • Grounding

Ground the body, settle the breath, and cultivate deep parasympathetic nervous system safety. Before expanding outward, we establish our roots.

R

REVEAL

Awareness • Patterns • Truth

Notice sensations, unconscious compensations, and emotional blocks without judgment. Curiosity replaces self-criticism as awareness deepens.

R

REWRITE

Agency • Boundaries • Self-Love

Consciously choose new internal narratives, dismantle obsolete survival mechanisms, and step into sovereign self-respect and intentional action.

I

INTEGRATE

Somatic Habits • Daily Life

Take the tools off the mat so transformation becomes daily physiological reality. Practice new responses in embodied, tangible, repeatable ways.

V

VOICE

Vocal Resonance • Dialogue • Clarity

Use breath, sound, affirmations, and honest reflective inquiry to speak your truth. Transformation happens when your voice matches your body.

E

EMBODY

Sovereignty • Compassion • Leadership

Live the work through posture, presence, ethical relationships, and purpose. Arrive fully into the life you are actively creating.

Backed by Lived Experience & Clinical Science

Every element of The Art of Arrival is intentionally engineered to regulate biology, hydrate fascia, and restore psychological sovereignty.

Vagal Tone & Nervous System

Intelligent breathwork stimulates the vagus nerve, shifting your autonomic system out of chronic sympathetic fight-or-flight into restorative parasympathetic repair.

Fascial Hydration & Joints

Somatic loading and multidimensional sequencing release tight fascia, decompress compressed vertebrae, and restore structural biomechanics without forced strain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you arrive.

Do I need to be advanced at yoga or flexible to join?

Not at all. "Every Body Is A Yoga Body" is our founding creed. The Art of Arrival Method is trauma-informed, zero-perfection, and built with individualized modifications for all bodies, mobility levels, and seasons of life.

Which airport should I fly into?

Fly into Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) in San José, Costa Rica. We coordinate group and private Sprinter shuttles directly from SJO to Amanti Sanctuary in Playa Hermosa.

Can I attend as a solo traveler?

Yes! More than 60% of our guests arrive solo. Our intentional opening circles and small-group atmosphere make connecting with aligned, high-vibe humans effortless and safe.

How does the $500 deposit work?

Your $500 deposit secures your room and spot in the retreat immediately via Stripe. The remaining balance can be settled in scheduled flexible payments prior to the retreat dates.

Can dietary restrictions (vegan, gluten-free) be accommodated?

Yes. Our chefs at SWAY prepare 100% customizable gourmet meals for Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, and detox preferences. Simply note any allergies upon checkout.
