The Arrive Yoga Method™

Founded by Carly Anne Kasinpila. A proprietary, trauma-aware yoga and leadership development methodology integrating classical Eastern yogic principles with modern neuroscience, somatic psychology, and conscious communication.

Foundational Philosophy

Five Pillars of Truth

At its core, the Arrive Yoga Method is anchored upon five immutable truths:

01. The Body Stores Experience

Emotions, trauma, habits, and beliefs live within connective tissues and fascia. Mindful movement is the true gateway to physical and emotional healing.

02. The Voice Is Part of the Practice

Self-expression is essential to lasting transformation. Vocal resonance, reflective journaling, and group dialogue are woven into every single session.

03. Discomfort Is a Teacher

When approached with curiosity and consent, challenge becomes growth rather than anxiety. We build resilience by feeling, not numbing.

04. Presence Creates Power

Autonomic regulation and body awareness are the twin foundations of unshakable confidence, clarity, and leadership in the world.

05. Healing Creates Leaders

Personal sovereignty naturally blossoms into compassionate, ethical leadership. When we heal ourselves, our communities thrive.

Curriculum Framework

The 6-Phase Class Architecture

Each Arrive Yoga session follows an adaptable, somatic architecture that balances rigor with restorative grace:

Phase 1

Arrival & Grounding

Conscious breath awareness, nervous system down-regulation, intention setting, and honest emotional check-in.

Phase 2

Alignment & Education

Anatomical cueing, joint stabilization, injury prevention, and building deep mind-to-fascia connectivity.

Phase 3

Flow & Strength

Dynamic, rhythm-infused vinyasa flow, functional strength building, balance, and endurance development.

Phase 4

Expansion & Exploration

Creative sequencing, individual bodily self-expression, optional challenges, and self-chosen variations.

Phase 5

Surrender & Integration

Restorative postures, guided meditation, somatic unwind, sound baths, and deep parasympathetic reset.

Phase 6

Voice & Reflection

Journaling prompts, partner inquiry, circle dialogue, and real-world mindset coaching integration.

Clinical Snapshot

Clinical Evidence Behind The Practice

YOGA & MENTAL HEALTH: Multiple systematic reviews published by NIH and NCBI demonstrate that consistent yoga practices down-regulate sympathetic arousal, significantly lower depressive scores, and stimulate vagal tone to support neuroplasticity.

FOREST BATHING & BIODIVERSITY: Peer-reviewed studies in environmental medicine reveal that airborne phytoncides in primary tropical rainforests stimulate human Natural Killer (NK) cell activity and significantly suppress serum cortisol levels for up to 30 days post-exposure.

COMMUNAL CONNECTION: Research from the World Health Organization (WHO) and the American Psychological Association (APA) confirms that authentic social bonds and shared vulnerability actively counteract loneliness — recognized today as a risk factor equivalent to chronic cardiovascular illness.
