The Vision & Ecosystem

Carly Anne Kasinpila is a multidimensional entrepreneur, wellness leader, creative strategist, and global retreat host dedicated to helping individuals and communities reconnect to their sovereign power, purpose, and voice. As Founder & CEO of Arrive Yoga & Wellness Center, Arrive Consulting, Yogis Give Back, and The Art of Arrival Retreats, Carly has built an ecosystem where mindfulness, financial intelligence, artistic expression, and social impact harmonize.

Roots, Music & Personal Foundation

Carly’s path is deeply grounded in creative resilience and community. She is the daughter of Howard “Barry” Appledorf, a pioneering American entrepreneur who helped popularize karaoke across the United States through his iconic company, Sing A Song & Karaoke Store in Hollywood, Florida. Growing up immersed in music, performance, and community taught Carly early on the profound power of giving people a platform to be seen, heard, and celebrated without judgment.

Her father’s international life in Thailand also exposed Carly to diverse cultural traditions and spiritual philosophies from a young age, cultivating her global perspective, cultural humility, and deep appreciation for mindfulness as a universal human language.

Teaching Journey: Warner Bros to Desert Mountains

Over the past decade, Carly has guided thousands of students across private sessions, studio classes, corporate leadership retreats, and major music festivals. Her diverse teaching milestones include:

  • Guiding meditation for high-level entertainment executives at Warner Brothers Studios in Los Angeles.
  • Leading large-scale yoga and acro-yoga workshops at premier music and arts festivals nationwide.
  • Teaching non-English speaking communities in Thailand through body awareness and movement.
  • Guiding midnight meditation for 40 practitioners on a sacred mountain in Israel under the stars.
  • Supporting private high-profile clients through trauma rehabilitation and nervous system restoration.

The Artist & Performer

In addition to her executive leadership, Carly is a multifaceted creative artist. She draws, paints large-scale acrylics, performs fire spinning, trains in aerial silks and acro-yoga, writes poetry, sings, and plays piano. As a professional model and actress, she has walked runway shows for New York Fashion Week (NYFW) and participated in commercial media productions.

To Carly, art is not an ornament — it is a form of radical truth-telling and somatic release.
