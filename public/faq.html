Questions, Answered

Retreat FAQ

Everything you want to know before you arrive — what's included, how payments work, what to pack, and how to get here. Still curious? Ask our concierge.

Who is this retreat for? +

This retreat is perfect for:

  • People seeking a reset without sacrificing beauty or comfort
  • Wellness-minded travelers ready for something deeper than a vacation
  • Anyone navigating a personal transition or season of growth
  • Those craving meaningful community and inspiration
  • Guests who want a balance of structure and freedom
Do I need yoga experience? +

Not at all. Every Body Is A Yoga Body is not just our slogan — it is how we teach. Classes are trauma-aware, accessible for all levels and body types, and built around breath, grounding, and honest self-inquiry rather than performance. You will never be asked to compete, compare, or push past your edge.

What is included in my package? +

Every package includes:

  • 5 nights / 6 days of luxury lodging at Amanti Sanctuary
  • All scheduled yoga, breathwork, and elemental workshops
  • Opening and closing ceremonies, group experiences, and rituals
  • Daily breakfast plus select shared meals (non-alcoholic beverages included)
  • Curated excursions aligned with the Earth, Water, Fire, and Air themes
  • Community support before and after the retreat, plus integration tools and resources
What is not included? +
  • Flights to and from Costa Rica
  • Travel insurance (strongly recommended)
  • Airport transfers (available as an add-on)
  • Spa services, unless selected as an add-on
  • Optional excursions and custom add-ons beyond the set package
  • Alcoholic beverages (available for purchase separately) and personal expenses
Are excursions included in the base package? +

Yes — curated excursions aligned with each elemental day are part of the experience. Beyond those, a full menu of optional adventures and spa services is available as add-ons, from waterfall rappelling to the Tortuga Island day trip. Explore the full adventure menu.

What airport do I fly into? +

Fly into Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) near San José, Costa Rica. Our team greets you on arrival day and our shuttles bring you to Amanti Sanctuary in Playa Hermosa. Flight guidance is available — just ask, and we'll help you find a cost-effective, time-efficient itinerary.

Is airport transportation included? +

Airport transfers are available as an add-on (approximately $150 per airport run for up to 4 guests, plus tax). All vehicles are air conditioned, and group shuttles are coordinated around arrival and departure days so nobody navigates alone.

Are payment plans available? +

Yes. A $500 non-refundable deposit reserves your spot, and the remaining balance is split into scheduled installments. Limited flexibility is available upon request — reach out and we'll find a rhythm that works.

What should I pack? +

Pack light and comfortable: breathable layers for practice, swimwear, sandals and walking shoes, sun protection, and a journal for reflection. A detailed packing list is sent to every guest after booking — including the all-white dress theme for Air day.

Is alcohol included? +

Meals and non-alcoholic beverages are included. Alcoholic beverages are not included but are available for purchase separately at the resort.

Can I attend solo? +

Absolutely — many of our guests arrive solo and leave with lifelong friends. Community is part of the curriculum here: opening circles, shared meals, and heart talks are designed so nobody stays a stranger for long.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Please review our full Terms & Cancellation Policy for deposit, refund, and transfer details before booking. Travel insurance is strongly recommended for every guest.
