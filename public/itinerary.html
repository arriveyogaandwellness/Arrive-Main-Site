The 6-Day Journey

"Five elements guiding you inward and upward — from somatic grounding to total expansion."

Each day is anchored in an elemental frequency, pairing trauma-informed somatic yoga with experiential workshops, curated excursions, and celebratory farm-to-table dining under the stars.

Reserve Spot ($500 Deposit) View Daily Breakdown
01

Day 1: EARTH — Arrival & Grounding

Earth Element: Anchor into the body & regulate nervous system

Color Theme: Green & Earth Neutrals

Daytime Flow

✈️ Arrival Window: Private air-conditioned shuttles pick up guests directly from Juan Santamaría Airport (SJO).

🍹 Welcome Reception: Refreshments, fresh coconut water, and light bites upon arriving at Amanti Sanctuary.

🛌 Orientation: Settle into private villa suites, unpack, and decompress from flight.

Evening Ceremony

5:00–6:00 PM: Meet & Greet Circle — Creating community connection through conscious communication.

6:30–7:30 PM: Themed Dinner: "Rooted in Abundance" prepared by SWAY chefs.

7:30 PM+: Beach Bonfire & Workshop: "From Root to Rise — The Foundations of Self".

Day 1 Highlight: Playa Hermosa Arrival & Grounding

Anchor your nervous system into Costa Rica's black sand beaches with mindful fire ceremony and connection circles.

02

Day 2: WATER — Seeds of Hope & Flow

Water Element: Fluid strength & emotional softening

Color Theme: Ocean Blues & Teals

Morning Practice & Adventure

7:00–8:00 AM: Fresh organic farm breakfast & cold-pressed juice bar.

8:00–9:00 AM: The Art of Arrival Vinyasa Flow on open-air Sky Decks.

10:00 AM–2:00 PM: Choose your excursion: Rainforest Waterfalls + Rainmaker Hanging Bridges, OR Playa Hermosa Black Sand Beach.

Afternoon Reset & Feast

2:00–3:00 PM: Poolside lunch, free time, and en-suite spa treatments.

3:30–5:00 PM: Workshop: Emotional Alchemy — Transforming Heavy Energy into Fluid Power.

7:00–8:30 PM: Themed Dinner: "Go With The Flow".

Day 2 Highlight: Poolside Reset & Waterfall Flow

Recharge with fresh tropical provisions and restorative pool waters after morning flow.

03

Day 3: FIRE — Ignite, Transform & Lead

Fire Element: Courage, breakthrough & sovereign passion

Color Theme: Crimson, Orange & Gold

Morning Fire & Adventure

7:00–8:00 AM: Breakfast at SWAY.

8:00–9:00 AM: Power Yoga & Functional Core Mobility.

9:30 AM–2:00 PM: Choose your fire adventure: Beach & Jungle Horseback Riding, Sacred Temazcal Sweat Lodge, or Monkey Mangrove Cruise.

Empowerment Workshop & Evening

2:00–3:00 PM: Wholesome organic lunch.

3:30–5:00 PM: Workshop: "Our Actions Have Consequences: Prevention → Pain → Power → Passion → Purpose".

7:00–8:30 PM: Themed Dinner: "Crouching Yogi, Hidden Lion".

Day 3 Highlight: Power Yoga & Functional Core

Move from prevention through pain to sovereign power under Carly's expert guidance.

04

Day 4: AIR — Inner Realms & Spacious Mind

Air Element: Breath mastery, mental spaciousness & Haute White Party

Color Theme: All-White Haute Couture

Pranayama & Canopy

7:00–8:00 AM: Breakfast on Café Terrace.

8:00–9:00 AM: Breathwork & Vocal Resonance Practice with Carly Anne.

9:30 AM–2:00 PM: Choose your air adventure: Ocean Canopy Ziplining OR Butterfly Garden Sanctuary Walk.

White Party & Wisdom

2:00–3:00 PM: Mediterranean lunch.

7:30–8:30 PM: Tapas Dinner: "It’s The Little Things That Count".

9:00 PM: Evening Workshop: "The Space Between Your Thoughts" (All-White Party).

"To ask 'What if I fall?' is secure yet restricting. To ask 'What if I fly?' is expansion."

Day 4 Highlight: Ocean-View Canopy & Sky Decks

Master breathwork and open your voice immersed in 360-degree views of the rainforest canopy.

05

Day 5: UNLOCK THE AVATAR STATE

Day 5 • Avatar State: Tortuga Island catamaran, Gratitude Gala & DJ Aphasia

Color Theme: Rainbow / Prismatic Spectrum

Island Catamaran Excursion

8:00 AM: Slow Somatic Flow & Guided Journaling.

9:15 AM: Early morning breakfast.

10:30 AM–4:00 PM: Full-Day Trip to Tortuga Island via catamaran. Turquoise waters, snorkeling with tropical sea turtles, island beach BBQ, and The Art of Arrival Island Circle.

Gratitude Gala & Celebration

7:00–8:00 PM: Final Feast: "Gratitude Gala" Celebration Dinner.

8:30 PM+: Closing Ceremony, Reflection Circle, and Live Music Performance by DJ Aphasia. Optional rooftop stargazing and night pool dip.

Day 5 Highlight: Gratitude Gala & Celebration

Private catamaran sailing to Tortuga Island followed by sunset DJ Aphasia feasting and celebration.

06

Day 6: DEPARTURE — What If I Fly?

Sunday Evening / Monday Departures • Taking the sanctuary into the world

7:30 AM: Gentle Morning Stretch & Heart Circle.

8:15 AM: Farewell Breakfast served at Café Terrace.

9:30 AM+: Group and private airport shuttles to Juan Santamaría Airport (SJO) begin as scheduled.

"You do not return to your old life the same. You return grounded, brave, and arrived."
