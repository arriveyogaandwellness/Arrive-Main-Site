Notice to Guests

By submitting a reservation deposit, completing registration through Stripe, or staying at Amanti Sanctuary for The Art of Arrival Retreat, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and voluntarily agree to be legally bound by these terms.

01. Reservation Deposits & Payment Schedules

A non-refundable deposit of $500.00 USD is required at the time of booking to secure room selection and participation in The Art of Arrival Retreat.

The remaining balance of the chosen package (Standard $2,200 or VIP $3,100, plus any selected room upgrades, excursions, or spa add-ons) must be settled according to your scheduled payment agreement prior to the retreat start date (the officially announced start date). If the balance is not completed on schedule, the organizer reserves the right to release the reserved room to waiting guests.

02. Guest Cancellation & Refund Policy

Due to boutique property commitments, private chef provisions, and third-party transport reservations:

  • The $500 deposit is strictly non-refundable under all circumstances.
  • Cancellations requested more than 45 days prior to arrival: Any payments made above the initial $500 deposit are refundable or transferable to a future retreat credit.
  • Cancellations requested within 45 days of arrival: All payments are 100% non-refundable due to contractual resort allocation.
  • No refunds or partial credits will be granted for late arrivals, early departures, missed meals, or unused excursions.

03. Travel Insurance Strongly Recommended

All international retreat guests are strongly urged to purchase comprehensive travel insurance covering trip cancellation, flight interruptions, baggage loss, emergency medical care, and medical evacuation. The Art of Arrival and Amanti Sanctuary are not liable for travel disruptions beyond our reasonable control.

04. Assumption of Risk & Physical Activities

Participation in yoga, breathwork, waterfall hiking, canopy ziplining, horseback riding, ATV exploration, and ocean catamaran excursions involves inherent physical risks. Guests voluntarily assume all risks associated with participation and affirm that they are physically capable of undertaking such activities or will opt out at their discretion.

05. Property Rules & Respectful Conduct

Amanti Sanctuary is a sacred container for healing and rest. Guests agree to honor silent hours between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM, respect fellow guests and retreat staff, and refrain from reckless conduct. Illegal substances are strictly prohibited. Guests are financially responsible for any deliberate damage to villa furnishings, property, or linens.

06. Photography & Media Consent

Professional photography and videography may occur during shared sessions, excursions, and ceremonies for retreat documentation and marketing. Guests who prefer not to appear in media are invited to notify the team upon arrival.

07. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with applicable laws governing private retreat hosting and hospitality in Puntarenas, Costa Rica and Florida, USA.
